Learn the system

How to use it, step by step

The essentials you'll need from day one.

01How do I connect my number?

In Admin → WhatsApp click "Connect number", then on your phone open WhatsApp → Linked devices → Link a device, and scan the code. Within a minute your chats start appearing, and your phone keeps working as usual.

02How do I add my team?

In Admin → Users add each agent by email and choose the pages they can see: view only or edit. They log in with a code sent to them on WhatsApp.

03How do I set my business type?

In Business profile pick your type of work (sales, tech support and more). The system adapts wording, fields and customer stages to your business.

04How do I connect a free AI provider?

Create a free account at Google AI Studio or Groq, copy the API key, and paste it into AI providers. The system tests it and shows you the available models.

01Replying to a customer

Open Chats, pick the conversation, and type your reply or press / for canned replies. Every reply is logged under the agent who wrote it.

02Search everything

Type a couple of letters in the search bar and suggestions appear: customers, numbers, messages, files. Search by name or part of a number.

03Groups

In Groups see members, track unanswered questions, and turn on welcomes and anti-spam (links and numbers from outside the team are deleted and the sender removed).

04Bulk sending

In Bulk sending pick a list and write your message. It goes out in batches with limits that protect your number from bans.

01How do I turn on the bot?

In Bot → On/Off choose a mode: Drafts (the bot writes, your team reviews and sends) or Auto (it replies on its own when confident). Start with drafts for a week.

02How do I teach the bot my business?

In Learning let it read your past chats, and add Q&As and files. Every time someone on the team edits a bot reply, it learns the style.

03Bot schedule

Set working hours and night-time quiet hours; each group can have its own schedule. If a team member joins the chat, the bot steps back.

04Hashtag commands

Like #menu for the command list and #ask to ask the bot in a group. Create your own in Bot → Commands.

05Product catalog

In Product catalog add your products with price and photo. The bot quotes prices from it and sends the photo of the product asked about.

01Customer profile

Every customer has a profile with their stage, details, team notes and a log of every reply and file sent. Change the stage from the profile or the follow-up board.

02Automatic follow-ups

Turn them on in the bot settings: the bot checks in with customers under follow-up every few days, at the hour you choose.

03Reports

A daily report, a weekly report on each agent's response time and neglected customers, and a bot reply quality report, all delivered on WhatsApp.

04Backups

Connect Google Drive in General settings and turn on daily backups. Your messages stay saved even if deleted from the phone.

FAQ

Got a question?

Do I need a new number or a WhatsApp Business account?

No. You link your current number (regular or Business) with a QR code, like WhatsApp Web, and your phone keeps working as usual.

Will the bot give my customers wrong answers?

The bot answers only from your company's knowledge. When unsure, it writes a draft for your team or hands over to an agent. You can test it before going live.

Can several agents work at the same time?

Yes. Each agent has their own login and permissions, and the system shows who replied to whom.

Does the bot understand slang and dialects?

Yes. It replies in the customer's dialect and understands voice messages too.

Will my old messages show up?

Every message is saved from the moment you connect, and older ones are pulled in as far as WhatsApp allows.

Could my number get banned?

The connection uses a QR code like WhatsApp Web, not Meta's official API, so your number remains subject to WhatsApp's rules. Normal use with customers is safe, and the system has bulk-sending limits that protect your number. Spam and unwanted mass messages can lead WhatsApp to restrict or ban a number, which is the number owner's responsibility, as stated in the terms of use.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, no contracts.
